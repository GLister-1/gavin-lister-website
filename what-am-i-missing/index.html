What Am I Missing?

You have built a strong career. You have experience, credibility and a track record. So why is your job search producing so little movement?

At senior level, the usual job-search approach often stops working as well as it once did. What Am I Missing? explains why, and what experienced professionals need to do differently.

Why the usual approach stops working

More applications, more recruiter conversations and more random networking do not necessarily lead to better opportunities at senior level.

What Am I Missing? looks at what changes as your career becomes more senior: how clearly the market understands your value, whether you are visible to the right people, and whether you can create access to opportunities that may never reach the advertised market.

Who this book is for

This book is for experienced professionals and senior leaders who know they have strong experience and a solid track record, but are finding that the usual job-search approach is producing less traction than expected.

It is particularly relevant if you are:

  • targeting senior roles where many opportunities are never advertised
  • relying heavily on recruiters or online applications
  • unsure how to explain your relevance in a changing market
  • having plenty of conversations but not enough real opportunity
  • trying to make your next move deliberately rather than simply take the first role that appears

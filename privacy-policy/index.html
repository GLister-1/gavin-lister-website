Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Lister Consulting respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website or interact with Lister Consulting, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information I may collect

I may collect personal information that you choose to provide, including your:

  • name

  • email address

  • telephone number

  • professional or career information

  • information you provide when booking a conversation, completing a diagnostic or assessment, downloading a resource, subscribing to email updates, or engaging Lister Consulting for advisory services.

I only collect information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide a service, or support our professional relationship.

How information is collected

Personal information may be collected when you:

  • contact me directly

  • book a conversation or appointment

  • subscribe to The Insider’s Edge

  • complete an online diagnostic or questionnaire

  • download or purchase a resource

  • engage Lister Consulting for career advisory services

  • otherwise provide information voluntarily.

Some information may also be collected automatically by the website or third-party services used to operate it, such as basic technical information about your device or browser.

How I use personal information

I may use your information to:

  • respond to enquiries

  • provide career advisory, assessment or related services

  • arrange and manage appointments

  • provide resources you have requested

  • send email updates where you have subscribed

  • administer payments or purchases

  • improve my services and website

  • meet legal, accounting or administrative obligations.

I do not sell personal information.

Third-party service providers

Lister Consulting uses selected third-party platforms to provide website and business services.

These may include services such as:

  • Kit for email subscriptions and communications

  • Calendly for appointment scheduling

  • ScoreApp for online diagnostics

  • Payhip for resource delivery and purchases

  • Birkman International and authorised Birkman systems where the Birkman Method is used as part of an engagement.

When you use these services, information may be processed or stored by the relevant provider in accordance with its own privacy practices.

Some service providers may store or process information outside Australia.

Birkman assessments

Where you choose to undertake a Birkman assessment as part of a Career Direction & Fit engagement, the information you provide through the Birkman questionnaire is processed through authorised Birkman systems.

The resulting reports are used for the purpose of providing personalised career direction and advisory support.

Email communications

If you subscribe to The Insider’s Edge, your name and email address may be stored by the email platform used to manage the subscriber list.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link included in each email.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect personal information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access or disclosure.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access and correction

You may contact me if you would like to:

  • ask what personal information I hold about you

  • request that information be corrected

  • request that information be deleted where appropriate

  • ask a question about how your information has been handled.

Privacy enquiries or concerns

If you have a question or concern about privacy or the way your personal information has been handled, please contact Lister Consulting using the details provided on the Contact page.

I will make reasonable efforts to respond promptly and resolve any concerns.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or privacy practices.

The current version will always be available on this website.

Career Direction & Fit

For experienced professionals who are not yet clear about what comes next.

Using the Birkman Method alongside structured career advisory, we explore your interests, needs, strengths, work style and career fit to help identify realistic and motivating directions for the next stage of your career.

Who it’s for

Professionals at a career crossroads, considering a significant change, or wanting a more informed way to evaluate possible directions.

Format

Complete the Birkman questionnaire online at your own pace, followed by a personalised Birkman Signature report and two career direction consultations of up to 90 minutes each.

Together, we use the findings to explore what motivates you, where your strengths are most likely to be valued, the work environments in which you are likely to thrive, and the career directions worth investigating further.

Hidden Market Strategy Intensive

For experienced professionals who know broadly where they want to go, but are not getting enough traction.

This is a focused advisory engagement designed to identify what is stalling the search and reset the strategy around positioning, recruiter use, market access and the conversations most likely to create opportunities.

Who it’s for

Professionals who have already started looking, may be applying for roles, talking to recruiters or networking, but are finding that the effort is not producing enough meaningful progress.

Format

Four advisory sessions of up to 60 minutes each over four to six weeks.

We use the time to diagnose where the search is getting stuck, sharpen your positioning, identify the highest-value changes to your market approach, and put a more focused plan into action.

This is intentionally practical and focused. It is not designed to provide ongoing support through the full transition.

Investment

AUD $1,250 including GST, payable in full on commencement.

Executive Transition — Until Landing

For senior professionals making a significant career move who want experienced strategic support throughout the transition.

This is an ongoing advisory engagement designed to stay with you as the transition unfolds.

The intensity of support changes as the transition develops. Early on, the focus is usually on strategy, positioning and market activation. As opportunities emerge, the emphasis shifts to helping you assess them, prepare well and make strong decisions.

Who it’s for

Senior professionals pursuing a significant move where the search may take time, opportunities may emerge unpredictably, and ongoing judgement and support are more valuable than a fixed number of coaching sessions.

Format

There is no set number of sessions.

We meet as needed throughout the active transition, with additional short calls or email support where a live opportunity or important decision warrants it.

The engagement continues until you accept your next substantive role, or for up to 12 months from commencement, whichever comes first.

Because the engagement is not session-based, there are no unused sessions or credits to carry forward.

Investment

The maximum investment is AUD $6,500 including GST.

Rather than paying the full amount upfront, the fee is staged so that if you land quickly you do not pay for extended support you do not need:

  • AUD $3,500 on commencement
  • AUD $1,500 after 30 days, if the transition is still active
  • AUD $1,500 after 60 days, if the transition is still active

Once the full fee has been paid, there are no further advisory fees through landing, within the 12-month engagement period.

Targeted Advisory

For experienced professionals who need focused help with a specific career challenge, live opportunity or short piece of work.

Not every situation needs a broader advisory engagement. Sometimes you simply need experienced input on a defined part of the process.

This might include:

  • CV or LinkedIn positioning
  • interview preparation
  • case-study or presentation preparation
  • recruiter strategy
  • evaluating a live opportunity
  • offer evaluation and negotiation
  • another clearly defined career need

Format

Scoped individually according to what you need.

This may be a single consultation or a short piece of focused work, depending on what you need.

Investment

Quoted according to scope.

International clients: Prices are shown in Australian dollars. For clients based outside Australia, GST treatment and the final fee will be confirmed before engagement.

Engagement timing: Each service has its own timeframe and structure. Where a service includes a defined engagement period, support is intended to be used within that period unless we agree otherwise in advance. Executive Transition — Until Landing is structured differently and continues while the transition is active, for up to 12 months.

Career Direction & Fit

For experienced professionals who have reached a career crossroads and want greater clarity about what comes next.

Using the Birkman Method alongside structured career advisory, we explore your interests, needs, strengths, work style and career fit to help identify realistic and motivating directions for the next stage of your career.

About the Birkman Method →

Who it’s for

Professionals who are unsure about their next move, considering a significant change, or wanting a more informed way to evaluate possible career directions.

Format

Complete the Birkman questionnaire online at your own pace, followed by a personalised, in-depth Birkman report and two career direction consultations of 60–90 minutes each.

Together, we use the findings to explore what motivates you, where your strengths are most likely to be valued, the work environments in which you are likely to thrive, and the career directions worth investigating further.

Investment

AUD $1,650 including GST, payable in full on commencement.

Executive Transition Advisory

For senior professionals who want ongoing strategic support through a significant career transition.

We work together on the issues that matter most — positioning, target market, recruiter strategy, hidden-market access, conversations, interviews and market feedback.

Who it’s for

Senior professionals and executives whose career has stalled, whose current strategy is not producing enough traction, and who want experienced strategic support rather than generic career coaching.

Format

Three months, with up to 9 advisory conversations of up to 60 minutes each, plus light-touch support between sessions for questions, decisions and recalibration.

Investment

AUD $3,750 including GST, if paid in full on commencement.
Or 3 monthly instalments of $1,350 including GST.

Hidden Market Strategy Intensive

For experienced professionals who know something in their current career strategy is not working and want focused expert diagnosis, strategic clarity and a practical next-step plan.

This is a shorter, more focused engagement designed to identify the main barriers to traction and reset your approach around positioning, target market, recruiter strategy and hidden-market access.

Who it’s for

Experienced professionals who want a focused reset because their current search is not producing enough traction, but who do not need or want a three-month advisory engagement.

Format

Two advisory conversations of up to 75 minutes each, focused on diagnosis, strategic direction and the first practical changes to positioning, market access and recruiter engagement.

Investment

AUD $1,250 including GST, paid in full on commencement.

International clients: Prices are shown in Australian dollars. For clients based outside Australia, GST treatment and the final fee will be confirmed before engagement.

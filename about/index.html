About

About Gavin

I’ve spent much of my career helping experienced professionals navigate change — but my understanding of career transition isn’t only professional. It’s personal.

I’ve moved countries twice without a job secured. I’ve also faced redundancy twice — once with no support, and once with a coach.

That second experience changed the way I thought about career transition. I learned how to build and use relationships differently, how opportunities emerge beyond the advertised market, and why experienced professionals often need a different strategy from the conventional job-search playbook.

Experience and perspective

Over more than two decades, I’ve worked with more than 3,000 professionals across industries and borders, helping people navigate career transition, career strategy and the hidden job market.

For many years I worked with Right Management, coaching executives and senior managers through career transition, training consultants across Asia Pacific and the Middle East, and advising organisations on outplacement and career-transition practice. I have also coached MBA and Executive MBA students through Melbourne Business School.

Earlier in my career I worked in management recruitment, completing hundreds of recruitment and selection assignments. That experience gave me a perspective from both sides of the market — how experienced professionals look for opportunity, and how organisations actually find and assess people.

My approach

Senior career transition is rarely just a job-search problem.

It is often a positioning, visibility and access problem.

Many senior opportunities begin through reputation, trusted relationships and well-timed conversations before a formal role exists. My work is about helping experienced professionals become clearer about the value they offer, more visible to the right people, and better positioned to enter those conversations.

Where greater clarity about direction is needed, I draw on the Birkman Method to help clients better understand their interests, needs, strengths, work style and career fit.

Who I work with

I typically work with accomplished professionals whose career move has stalled, whose current approach is producing too little traction, or who sense that the rules of the market have changed.

They usually don’t need more generic job-search advice.

They need clearer positioning, better access to the market, stronger professional relationships and a more deliberate way of creating their next opportunity.

No hustle. No need to perform for the market.
Just intelligent, relationship-driven career leverage.
